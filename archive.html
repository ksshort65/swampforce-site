Congressional Scorecard
Both parties failed. They do not represent the American people.

The Republic

They forgot who they work for. Clean hands. The charter.

  • They forgot who they work for.
    Congress is paid by the people. One leader said he wants to break the people's spirit.
  • They keep their hands clean.
    Careful words. A crowd takes the hint. Cities burn. Insurers counted a billion. The Member still has the chair.
  • The republic, not the caption
    We the People. Not we the panel. Not we the clip.
  • They hold it by the blade
    They did not repeal the Constitution. They learned to use it as a weapon — the clause that shields them, never the duty that binds them.
  • They want a new Constitution.
    A group running as Democrats wrote it down: replace this country. November 3 is the last easy stop.

The Job

$7.3 billion for a part-time floor. $40 trillion on the meter.

  • The $7 billion machine
    FY2026 legislative branch: $7.258 billion. Salary is the decoy. The perks are the bill.
  • The debt they will not close
    They have not finished a budget on time since Clinton. Forty trillion. An unread pile is a door for fraud.
  • What they are protecting
    Chemonics. The Chamber. K Street. Soros money in local races. $200 for a late trade. That is the business.
  • Not a part-time job
    House: about 150 days a year. A third of those sessions under five minutes. No part-time job on earth pays like this. Full time, or the perks stop.
  • Why the lobby should be illegal
    Paid influence is not speech. It is a second government the people did not hire.
  • The recess blockade
    Pro forma gavels. Fake sessions. A president who cannot appoint. Article II, gutted on purpose.
  • What we can do
    Politics will not fix a Congress that writes its own rules. A united people, peaceful, on the record — or it does not happen.

The Hearing

The noise. The split. Full time or go home. The whole bill.

  • The noise.
    Congress talks for a living. The country still does not get a budget. Words without a ledger are the job they invented to keep the job.
  • They sold the split.
    Politicians and the panel have no policy, so they sell a neighbor to hate. Division is the product. The statute never makes the caption.
  • Full time or go home.
    $7.258 billion. A floor that sits fewer days than a school year. Work the hours or resign.
  • The whole bill.
    A budget the country can read. Every reconciliation print. The stack, not the clip. How the stack is forced.
  • It does not fit.
    Medicare for All is not a slogan. It is $32–34 trillion in extra federal spending in ten years — more than a full year of everything America produces — on a Treasury that already cannot close a $1.9 trillion hole.
  • The check they will not write.
    Reparations returns every election because it is a loyalty test that never has to clear a bank. The advocates’ own number is $10–16 trillion. The Treasury does not have it. The Constitution does not permit a race-line from the IRS.

The Clip

A caption is not the tape. Play the minutes they cut.

  • They clipped the tape
    A cut sentence is how a country is taught a crime. Read the caption against the recording.
  • How did we get here
    Honest people kept the country standing while a political class learned to treat them as a tap. This is the story of that bargain, and of the man who broke it.
  • They don't debate. They flag.
    When they cannot beat the tape, they smear the person holding it.
  • A caption cannot be outlawed
    The rhetoric is the abuse. A hate law written by the same people would be the next clip.
  • The floor, not the feed
    Speech or Debate is a shield for the chamber. It is not a license for the rant. They write the emotion to stay inside the First Amendment.
  • The law they don't mention
    Most people were left with a cartoon of how a bill becomes law. The real sequence is longer, and the catch is usually in the annex.
  • How to watch a president
    Pause the clip. Name the job. Read down the names.

The Search

Find them. Who got paid. Defund ICE is the tell.

  • Find them.
    Three hundred thousand children are still a missing-persons file. This journal stands with them. Anyone blocking the search has chosen a side.
  • Who got paid.
    Up to $13 billion in one year. Cartels. Bought lanes. A bank indictment. The door was the product.
  • Defund ICE is the tell.
    The party that lost the children now wants the search abolished. Out of office. Off the ballot. That compass does not get a chair.

The Correction

Criminals walk. Taxes house illegal immigrants. The docket.

  • They let criminals go free.
    A cop is dead. The judge who let the man out is still on the bench. This is why the street feels like a siege.
  • They're spending taxpayer money on illegal immigrants.
    Hotels. Debit cards. Clothes. Federal law already said no. Governors did it anyway.
  • How a dirty list goes to court.
    Congress cannot be sued for being Congress. A dirty voter list into court in 90 days. Step by step.
  • Who to call.
    Real groups. Real addresses. Not a stranger on a video asking for a money.
  • This Congress cannot police itself
    Replace them. Term-limit them. They do not get to write their own privileges. A citizen board that also goes home.

The Ballot

A barcode is not a lock. Citizenship to vote was the tell.

  • A barcode is not a lock.
    The same USPS they called sabotaged in August they called the courier of the most secure election in history in November. The extra security they now advertise is a sorting sticker. Make it make sense.
  • The line in the sand
    The SAVE Act was the tell. Citizenship to vote. They killed it anyway.

The Parties

How the House was captured. What each party became.

  • How the House was captured
    Not a movie syndicate. A paying club. Both parties kept the books.
  • What the Democrats became
    A party of farmers and unions became a party of campuses, agencies, and clips.
  • What the GOP became
    Lincoln’s party became a donor club that talks founding and funds the swamp.
  • The uniparty mirror
    Cameras on: a fight. Cameras off: the same surveillance bill, the same blank check.

The Target

When a donor became a problem they went after the country.

  • Why they hate Trump
    They loved the donor. They turned when he stopped writing checks and started closing the problems the political club lived on. Then came the hoaxes — on the taxpayer dime.
  • The gauntlet
    Overlapping cases, a rifle, and a newscast. The point was to make a revolt look like a fever.
  • What he told them
    Globalists at the UN. The Taliban on the lawn. Iran in public. Not a staffer’s memo.
  • The word that never made the docket
    The word ran all day on television. It never appeared on the indictment. Here is the docket, the bodycam, and the testimony that does not match.
