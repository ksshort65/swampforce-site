Both parties failed. Here is what actually moves.

Congress proved it cannot police itself. The oath should mean something. It is not a citizen lawsuit and it is not a street. The Constitution already named the tools.

01 · The record

Both parties failed. They do not represent the American people. No balanced budget since Clinton. The twelve money bills do not pass. That open book is the fraud door. Vote the record, not the jersey.

02 · The neighbor is not the enemy

Congress is. Warfare talk is the cover for a job they will not do. House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries: “maximum warfare, everywhere, all the time.” Rep. Michael McCaul, leaving after twenty-two years: “You’re elected to fight and kill the other side.” Division is the product. The unread bill is the business.

Jeffries — C-SPAN, April 22, 2026 · McCaul — Fox News Radio, July 12, 2026

03 · They do not read the bills

Lobbyists write them. A handshake in a hallway becomes law. December 2020: 5,593 pages, about $2.3 trillion. CARES, about $2.2 trillion. Then the auditor counted. GAO: $100 billion to $135 billion in unemployment-insurance fraud. SBA Inspector General: about $200 billion in potentially fraudulent PPP and EIDL loans. Convictions in the thousands. Forfeiture around a billion. $5 billion in lobbying in 2025. That is not petition. That is a second government with a business card.

Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021 · CARES Act · GAO — UI fraud $100–135B · GAO — hundreds of billions

04 · The form letter

A constituent writes. A machine answers. The letter talks and says nothing. Pew, 2023: 87 percent want term limits. Pew, 2025: 83 percent want photo ID to vote. Gallup, 2024: 84 percent. The House passed the SAVE Act. The Senate sat on it. A petition is not a law. They will not pass what the country already settled.

Pew — 87% term limits, 2023 · Pew — 83% voter ID, 2025 · H.R. 22 — SAVE Act, passed House 220–208

05 · The date

The House is hired for two years. That is the firing. There is no federal recall. The Constitution left it out. A state cannot add it. The chamber may expel by two-thirds. They almost never will. In a safe district November is a ceremony. The primary is the contest.

Article I — terms and expulsion · Powell v. McCormack (1969) · U.S. Term Limits v. Thornton (1995)

06 · Full time or go home

Member salary: $174,000. Pension. Federal health insurance. A million-dollar office. No part-time job in America pays like that. They write their own pay. The 27th Amendment only delays a raise. It does not shrink a paycheck they do not earn. Cut the pay to match the hours. Same laws they write. Money bills on time. No giant unread bill.

2 U.S.C. § 4501 — Member pay · 27th Amendment · P.L. 119-37 — $7.258 billion legislative branch

07 · Keep the purse in Article I

Congress holds the money so a president is not a king. Handing the purse to the White House is not a reform. It is a throne. The failure is they will not do the job and will not police the people who hold it.

Article I, Section 9 — money by law

08 · Use a statute that exists

Dirty voter rolls: ninety-day notice, then district court. Trafficking: United States Attorney. Those have elements. A caption is not an indictment. This journal will not invent one. The oath is a job condition, not a citizen lawsuit. The 14th Amendment’s disqualification is for insurrection after the oath — force against the United States — not a press conference.

52 U.S.C. § 20510 — NVRA private action · 5 U.S.C. § 3331 — the oath · 14th Amendment, Section 3

09 · Print the tape

Peaceful. On the record. Independent. No PAC. Not a call to violence. The country is the employer. The neighbor is not on the payroll. Congress is. The legal firing date is Election Day. The record is so that date is not wasted on a jersey.
